### Why each piece is there

| Element | Why |
|---|---|
| Opening hook | Names the exact micro-moment the buyer recognizes ("Und was machen wir jetzt?"). LinkedIn algorithm rewards posts that get **dwell time in the first 1.7 seconds** - a concrete question beats a generic claim. |
| "Drei Stunden pro Kunde" | Anchors the cost in time, not money. Agency owners convert hours to billable rates themselves. |
| Bullet list with arrow glyphs | LinkedIn renders bullet emojis cleanly; the `→` glyph reads as "this is a deliverable" not "this is a feature." |
| "Was es nicht ist" section | The audit's strongest signal was that buyers Google *"seo reporting tool für agenturen"* - they want a clear *category* statement. Naming what it's not is the fastest way to land what it is. |
| "Datenkern in Deutschland" | Pulled straight from the homepage `agency-homepage.content.ts`. DACH agencies treat DSGVO as a real filter. |
| Pilot lock framing (10 agencies, 12 months) | Scarcity + price-lock matches the homepage offer exactly. Reinforces the same promise; don't undercut. |
| Two URLs (cal.com + topic page, not homepage) | Cal.com link captures bookings. The second link sends PageRank-curious traffic to `/de/seo-reporting-tool-agenturen` - the page already ranking weakly for the target queries - instead of the bare homepage. |
| Hashtag block at end | German + English hashtags. DACH agency community uses both. Limit to 7 - LinkedIn now penalizes >10. |

## Brand voice notes (applied)

- **Tone**: founder-led honest, concrete, no superlatives - matches `agency-homepage.content.ts` register ("Der Pilot filtert bewusst", "Kein Ersatz für deine SEO-Tools", "Limited to 10 qualified agencies").
- **No emojis** in body except the `→` glyph (which reads as a list arrow, not decoration).
- **First-person singular** ("Ich öffne den Pilot…") - founder-led pilots need the founder's voice, not "we."
- **No "we help X do Y" construction** - that's SaaS-marketing default and the homepage deliberately avoids it. Used "Ich baue", "WebHealth sitzt über…" instead.
- **Anti-claim**: "Was es nicht ist" - both the audit and the homepage lead with disqualification. The post mirrors that.

## SEO/discoverability notes

- LinkedIn posts are **indexed by Google**, especially when they have inbound engagement. A high-engagement LinkedIn post can rank for branded queries like *"WebHealth pilot"* or *"founder-led SEO reporting tool"* - secondary SEO benefit beyond the direct referral traffic.
- The two links inside the post are **`rel="nofollow"`** on LinkedIn - they will not pass PageRank directly. The value here is: (a) qualified referral traffic, (b) brand recognition for direct searches later, (c) potential re-shares to non-LinkedIn properties that *do* link follow.
- The English variant cross-posted to Twitter/X gives a second indexable surface - Twitter links are also nofollow but have a faster discovery path.
