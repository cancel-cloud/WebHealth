## Hard rules

1. **Never reuse a post.** Each one is one-shot. If you need more, regenerate from this folder, do not paraphrase.
2. **No emoji.** Lukas voice — direct, polite, no hype.
3. **No invented results.** The pilot has zero results yet. Don't add testimonials, case studies, or made-up percentages.
4. **No claim-safety violations.** Do not introduce: "recover lost traffic", "AI fixes your SEO", "guaranteed rankings", "exactly why traffic dropped", "lost clicks due to X".
5. **One language per post.** German for DACH-targeted, English for everything else. Do not mix in the same post.
6. **Founder voice.** "I", not "we". DevBrew is the company, but the pilot is run by Lukas personally.
7. **Cal link discipline.** The bare URL `https://cal.com/lukas-devbrew/kennenlernen` only — no UTM parameters, no shortener, no tracking suffix.

## Account-safety notes

The previous account got suspended for repetitive content. Every post in this folder is unique in **angle, opening sentence, structure, and CTA framing**. Keep it that way:

- Do not paraphrase one post and call it a new one.
- Do not split a post into "part 1 / part 2" threads.
- Do not auto-schedule the same post in DE and then EN — they will look like translations even if the wording differs.
- If a post gets flagged or shadowbanned, retire it. Do not retry a softened version.

## When to regenerate

- The pilot count changes ("0 of 10 booked" → "3 of 10"). Post 5 in `posts-x.md` is the one to update; same for any future Reddit comments referencing the count.
- A new product fact lands (price change, feature shipping, lock period changes).
- After any post is flagged, rate-limited, or removed.
- After every 14-day posting cycle — write fresh posts rather than reusing the second half of the file.
