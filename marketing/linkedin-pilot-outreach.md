# LinkedIn Pilot Outreach — WebHealth

**Target:** Small SEO agencies, SEO freelancers, digital agencies managing client sites.
**Goal:** Get them into the WebHealth pilot round.
**Style:** Hormozi Grand Slam — specific dream outcome, low effort, time-compressed, risk-reversed. Connect first (no pitch). Pitch the lead magnet on reply.
**Sender:** Lukas (DevBrew) — `lukas@devbrew.dev`
**Pilot URL:** https://webaudit-25.localcan.dev

---

## Targeting filter (who to send to)

Send only to people whose profile shows at least one of:
- "SEO consultant / freelancer / specialist"
- "Founder/Owner of a small SEO or digital agency (1–15 people)"
- "Head of SEO / SEO Lead at an agency"
- Posts about client reporting, GSC, GA4, SEO audits, monthly retainers

Skip: in-house SEO at large brands, pure link-builders, affiliate SEOs, tool vendors.

- Specific mechanism: GSC + GA4 + crawl → prioritized actions
- Low effort: just a connect, no call ask
- Honest frame ("not pitching") = pattern interrupt vs. typical SDR spam

**"Send me more info first"** →

**"Not interested"** →
> All good — appreciate the honesty. If it'd help, I can share the monthly-action framework on its own as a Notion doc, no pilot required. Up to you.

(That last one keeps the door open and the relationship warm — small ascending offer.)

## Sending cadence

- Day 0: send connect (Message 1)
- Day 0 (on accept): send Message 2 same day, within 2 hours of accept if possible
- Day +3: if no reply → soft bump: *"No rush {{First}} — just bumping this so it doesn't get buried. Worth me sending the pilot over?"*
- Day +10: if still no reply → close loop: *"Sounds like timing's off — closing your slot for now, but happy to reopen if you want it later."* (the takeaway / scarcity move)
- Stop after that. Don't be the 6-message-follow-up person.

## Volume target for the pilot round

- 10 seats to fill
- Connect accept rate ~30% on cold ICP-matched LinkedIn
- Reply rate on Message 2 in this format ~25–35%
- Pilot conversion from reply ~40–60% (it's free + done-for-you)

→ Send **~120–180 connect requests** to fill 10 seats. Spread over 2 weeks to stay under LinkedIn's weekly invite cap (~100/wk).
