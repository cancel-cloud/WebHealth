## Legal-safe outreach rule

For German cold outreach, treat LinkedIn DMs as potentially covered by electronic advertising rules. Keep invite notes and first messages non-promotional:

- No offer.
- No pilot mention.
- No link.
- No demo request.
- No discount/free-seat language.

Only mention WebHealth/pilot after the person accepts and replies with a real interest signal.
