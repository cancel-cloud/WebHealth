## TL;DR

WebHealth is **two weeks into Google's index**. GSC shows 24 impressions, 0 clicks across 8 queries on 3 pages; GA4 shows 14 active users, all of them Direct or Referral (zero organic). This is not a CTR or ranking problem yet — it is an **indexation, discovery, and authority** problem. None of the "Top Priority Actions" in this report try to move CTR from 1% to 3%; they try to get the remaining 8 sitemap URLs indexed, fix one canonical conflict that *is* costing impressions, link the topic page so it stops being orphan-shaped, and start the authority work that has to happen before any of the targeted DACH agency queries become reachable.

Be warned: with only 24 impressions across 28 days, every metric below has high variance. The recommendations are grounded in the **code and structure** of the site, not in noisy GSC averages.

### #1 — Submit the sitemap to GSC and request indexing on the 8 missing pages
🔴 Critical · **Impact**: ~3-5× the impression base (everything else is gated by this) · **Effort**: Low (~30 min)

**What**: The `apps/web/src/app/sitemap.ts` defines 11 URLs (`/de`, `/en`, `/de/seo-reporting-tool-agenturen`, `/en/seo-reporting-software-agencies`, `/about`, `/pricing`, `/demo`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/impressum`, `/dpa`) but GSC has only ever shown 3 of them (`/de/seo-reporting-tool-agenturen`, `/impressum?lang=de`, `/de`). The English variants, pricing, demo, and about page have **zero impressions** — Google has not indexed them.

**Evidence**: `Seiten.csv` lists exactly 3 URLs with non-zero impressions. The sitemap.xml route exists in code but the daily impressions chart (`Diagramm.csv`) shows discovery began only May 6 — Google found the property but has not yet crawled the long tail.

**Fix**:
1. Open GSC → *Sitemaps* → submit `https://webhealth.devbrew.dev/sitemap.xml`.
2. For each of the 8 unindexed URLs, paste into the URL inspection tool and click **"Request Indexing"**. Prioritize `/en/seo-reporting-software-agencies`, `/en`, `/pricing`, `/demo`, `/about`.
3. Add a `lastModified: new Date()` per page (already done in `sitemap.ts` ✅) and a real `lastmod` from git history once content stabilizes.

**Why it works**: With this little traffic and authority, Google won't aggressively crawl on its own. Manual indexing requests are the cheapest way to multiply the impression base.

### #2 — Fix the canonical/sitemap conflict on legal pages (impressum, privacy, terms, dpa) — the German variants
🔴 Critical · **Impact**: Currently the only consistently ranking page (`/impressum?lang=de` pos 5.2) is on a query-param URL that the sitemap doesn't list · **Effort**: Medium (1–2 hours of refactor + redirects)

**What**: In `apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/legal/legal.ts` (line 35) the German legal page sets `canonical: "/impressum?lang=de"` and `alternates.languages.de: "/impressum?lang=de"`. But the `sitemap.ts` lists only `/impressum` (no query string). Google has currently chosen `/impressum?lang=de` (5 impressions, position 5.2) — but the sitemap is telling it to canonicalize to the non-query version.

**Evidence**:
- `Seiten.csv`: `/impressum?lang=de` has 5 impressions at position 5.2 (the site's best position by far).
- Code: `legal.ts` line 35 (`localePath = locale === "de" ? "${path}?lang=de" : path`), `sitemap.ts` lines for `/impressum` (no query string), `legal.test.ts` line 35 asserts the `?lang=de` pattern.
- The conflict: canonical says X, sitemap says Y. Google currently picks X but this is fragile and signals split if anyone links to `/impressum` (English).

**Fix** — pick one of two paths:

**Option A (recommended, larger refactor)**: Convert legal pages to path-based locales: `/de/impressum`, `/en/impressum`, `/de/privacy`, etc. This is consistent with how marketing pages already work (`/de`, `/en`, `/de/seo-reporting-tool-agenturen`). Update `sitemap.ts` to include both language paths, `legal.ts` to drop the `?lang=` pattern, add 301s from `/impressum?lang=de` → `/de/impressum`.

**Option B (small, today)**: Add the `?lang=de` URLs to the sitemap so the canonical and sitemap agree:
```ts
// in sitemap.ts
{ url: `${BASE_URL}/impressum`, ... },
{ url: `${BASE_URL}/impressum?lang=de`, ... },
// repeat for privacy, terms, dpa
```

**Why it works**: Position 5.2 for impressum suggests Google trusts the page for branded German queries. Stabilize that signal now, before the property accumulates more conflicting indications.

### #3 — Link the SEO topic page from the homepage (it's currently orphaned in HTML)
🟡 High · **Impact**: 2–4× the impressions on `/de/seo-reporting-tool-agenturen` (already 19/28 days = the site's best-performing page) · **Effort**: Low (~20 min — single component edit)

**What**: `/de/seo-reporting-tool-agenturen` is the page Google is showing for *all* of the targeted DACH agency queries ("seo reporting software für agenturen", "seo-reporting-tools für agenturen", "seo plattform für agenturen mit reporting funktion", "seo reporting monatlich"). But `grep` across `agency-homepage-client.tsx` and `agency-header.tsx` shows **zero anchor links from the homepage to this page**. It only exists in `sitemap.ts`, `agency-seo.logic.ts`, and `agent-discovery.logic.ts`. From a PageRank-flow perspective the page is orphaned — Google can find it via sitemap but no on-site link tells it the page matters.

**Evidence**:
- `apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/agency-homepage-client.tsx` (1221 lines) has internal Link components only to `/demo`, `/dashboard`, and external `cal.com` — never to the topic-page slug.
- `Seiten.csv`: topic page already pulls 19 of 24 total impressions (79%) at position 62 — and that's *despite* being orphaned in-content.

**Fix**: Add at least two in-content links from the DE homepage to `/de/seo-reporting-tool-agenturen` (and likewise EN → `/en/seo-reporting-software-agencies`). Suggested placements:
1. The **"Positioning"** section ("Kein Ersatz für deine SEO-Tools. Die Entscheidungsschicht darüber.") — add a sentence like *"Mehr zum Konzept: [SEO Reporting Tool für Agenturen — wie aus Monatsreports Maßnahmen werden](/de/seo-reporting-tool-agenturen)."*
2. The **"Erster Snapshot / First snapshot"** section — link the heading or the question "Welche Queries sind nah an besseren Positionen?" out to the topic page.
3. A footer "Learn more" link in `marketing-shell.tsx` (`apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/marketing-shell.tsx`) that always points to the topic page in the active language.

**Why it works**: The topic page already has the right metadata (title in `AGENCY_SEO_TITLES`, JSON-LD via `AgencyJsonLd`, hreflang via `getAgencyLanguageAlternates("topic")`). What it's missing is the in-content link signal that tells Google "this is a hub page, not a dead end." With ~62 avg position, even one descriptive internal link from the highest-PageRank page on the site (the homepage) should be enough to start the climb.

### #4 — Build the first 5 quality backlinks (you have ~zero today)
🟡 High · **Impact**: Necessary precondition to rank in the top 20 for "seo reporting tool für agenturen" type queries · **Effort**: Medium (1–2 days outreach work)

**What**: The GA4 acquisition data shows 2 referral users out of 10 new users — almost no inbound link signal. For commercial DACH queries like *"seo reporting software für agenturen"* the SERP is dominated by Semrush, Sistrix, Ahrefs, Agencyanalytics, Looker Studio. These competitors have hundreds of referring domains. WebHealth has roughly enough referring signal to be discovered, but not to rank.

**Evidence**:

- No off-page authority work was visible in the code (no `rel="me"` profile links, no documented partnerships, no link to a parent DevBrew brand page from the homepage footer).
- The NickLaunches badge in the footer (`marketing-shell.tsx`) is one off-page signal — keep it.

**Fix** — the **first five** I would chase in this order:
1. **DevBrew main site footer/products page** — add `https://webhealth.devbrew.dev` link from `devbrew.dev` (same-org but still a real link). Verify with `site:devbrew.dev webhealth`.
2. **OMR Reviews** (https://omr.com/reviews) — German equivalent of G2; the DACH agency community uses it. List WebHealth in "SEO Reporting" / "Marketing Reporting" categories.
3. **Capterra DE** + **GetApp DE** — same play, free listings.
4. **ProductHunt** — submit during EU launch window (Tuesday 09:00 PT). The homepage already markets the "Founding Agency Pilot" angle which is PH-friendly.
5. **One guest post or "comparison post" on a friendly publisher** — e.g., LinkedIn or a partner agency's blog: "Why we picked X for monthly client reporting." Avoids the cold-pitch loop.

Skip for now: paid link-building, low-quality directory dumps, reciprocal-link schemes. These will hurt more than help at this property size.

**Why it works**: Position 60–90 on commercial keywords with no referring domains is the predicted state. Position improvements below 30 require either (a) much better content depth than competitors *or* (b) authority signals. (b) is cheaper for a 2-week-old site.

## What to Ignore (For Now)

- **CTR optimization**. 0/24 over 7 active days is noise. Revisit once any page reaches position 20 with >100 impressions.
- **Mobile-specific fixes**. 2 mobile impressions = not enough signal. Performance audits for mobile are still worth doing as engineering hygiene, but they will not move SEO rankings at this stage.
- **The `semrush report` impression (6 imp, pos 33.5)**. It's not a query you want to compete on — it's a competitor-brand query. Google is matching it loosely because the page mentions "Semrush." Don't optimize for it; don't try to suppress it. It will fade as more relevant queries take hold.
- **Country expansion beyond DACH**. NO/US impressions are 3 combined; everything in copy and JSON-LD says DACH-first. Don't dilute.
- **Search Appearance rich-result tracking** (`Darstellung in der Suche.csv` is empty). Will populate naturally once average position improves; nothing to fix in code right now besides the schema additions noted above.
